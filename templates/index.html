Why this system exists

Closing the gap between reporting a crime and seeing it through

Victims are still turned away at the counter, and once a docket is opened there is often no reliable record of who handled it or where it stands.

1,144+police stations required to register every valid complaint (National Instruction 3 of 2011)
658case dockets confirmed missing nationally over a five-year period
100%of actions on a docket written to an append-only audit trail

How it works

One docket, five accountable steps

Every step is time-stamped and linked to the person who performed it.

1. Check in

Enter your ID number at the kiosk when you arrive.

Complainant

2. Register

An officer captures your case and a docket number is issued.

CSC Official

3. Assign

The commander assigns a detective and sets the priority.

Station Commander

4. Investigate

Suspects, witnesses, statements and evidence are added.

Detective

5. Close

The commander reviews the work and approves closure.

Station Commander
Features

Built for accountability at every stage

Designed around the two failures the system sets out to fix: unlawful refusals and lost or stalled dockets.

Kiosk check-in

Complainants record their presence before meeting an officer, so no visit goes unrecorded.

Structured refusal logging

If a case is not opened, a reason must be chosen and is saved with the officer's ID and time.

Full lifecycle tracking

Follow every docket from registration to assignment, investigation and closure.

Tamper-proof audit trail

Every action is appended to a log that no one, not even an administrator, can edit or delete.

Role-based access

Each user sees only what their role requires, with multi-factor sign-in for privileged users.

Dashboards & escalation

Commanders see refusals, docket ageing and stalled cases, with automatic alerts.
